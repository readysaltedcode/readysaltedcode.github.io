# Competitor Pricing Research
## UK Educational Resources & STEM Workshops

## 1. School Resource Subscriptions (Annual Licenses)

### Computing-Specific Schemes

| Provider | Product | Pricing | Notes |
|----------|---------|---------|-------|
| **Kapow Primary** | Computing Scheme | £215-350+VAT/year | Tiered by pupil numbers (0-150: £215, 150-300: £287, 300+: £350) |
| **Switched On Computing** (Rising Stars) | Year group packs | £150/year group | Includes teacher book, CD-ROM, posters |
| **Purple Mash** | Full platform | £450+/year | + £150 one-off setup fee |

### General Curriculum Providers

| Provider | Product | Pricing | Notes |
|----------|---------|---------|-------|
| **White Rose Maths** | Primary subscription | £210-385/year | Tiered by users (1-10: £210, 11-20: £260, 21-50: £325, 51-100: £385) |
| **White Rose Maths** | Individual teacher | £48/year | Single year group access |
| **Twinkl** | Individual Ultimate | ~£96/year (£7.99/mo) | School pricing requires quote |
| **TES Resources** | Per-resource | Free to £100+ | School license add-on available |

### Key Insights
- **Single subject subscriptions**: £150-450/year typical range
- **Tiered pricing by school size** is standard practice
- **MAT/Trust discounts** commonly offered (10-20% off)
- **Free trials** (7-14 days) are expected
- **CPD included** adds value and justifies higher pricing

## 3. Online Courses & CPD

### Free Options (Competition)
- **Barefoot Computing**: Free (DfE funded) - 100,000+ teachers trained
- **Teach Computing (NCCE)**: Free/bursaried - Government funded
- **Apple Teacher**: Free with certificate
- **Microsoft Educator Centre**: Free with badges
- **Google for Education**: Free certification

### Paid CPD Courses

| Provider | Format | Pricing | Notes |
|----------|--------|---------|-------|
| **High Speed Training** | Online courses | £20-100 per course | CPD accredited |
| **STEM Learning** | Subscriptions | Quote-based | Unlimited CPD model |
| **NEU (National Education Union)** | CPD courses | £50-200 | Member pricing available |

### Key Insights
- **Heavy free competition** from big tech and government
- **Paid courses need clear differentiation**: Certification, specialist topics, cohort-based
- **Freemium works**: Free intro → Paid certification/advanced
- **School bulk purchasing** preferred over individual teacher sales

## 4. Recommended Pricing for readysaltedcode

### Licensable Resources (Hero Product)

| Product | Recommended Price | Rationale |
|---------|------------------|-----------|
| **Single Lesson Pack** (e.g., Gemma Wearables) | £49-79 | Lower than Kapow single subject, accessible entry |
| **Subject Bundle** (all 6 lessons + materials) | £149-199/year | Competitive with Kapow £215 |
| **Premium Bundle** (lessons + video CPD + assessments) | £249-299/year | Added value justifies premium |
| **MAT License** (up to 10 schools) | £999-1,499/year | ~50% discount vs individual |

### Free Resources (Lead Generation)
- Sample lessons (1-2 per topic)
- Printable worksheets
- Getting started guides
- Newsletter with teaching tips

### Workshops (Max 5 days/month)

| Workshop Type | Recommended Price | Notes |
|---------------|------------------|-------|
| **Half-day workshop** (up to 30 pupils) | £350-450 | Competitive with STEMPOINT |
| **Full-day workshop** (up to 60 pupils) | £550-700 | Below premium providers |
| **Hackday** (full day, 30-60 pupils) | £600-800 | Premium format, unique offering |
| **Teacher CPD session** (half day) | £300-400 | In-school delivery |

### Travel Policy
- Within 50 miles: Included
- 50-100 miles: +£50
- 100+ miles: +£100 or overnight +£150

## 6. Competitive Positioning

### Price Position: **Mid-Market Quality**

| Segment | Competitors | readysaltedcode Position |
|---------|-------------|-------------------------|
| Budget | TES individual resources, free NCCE | NOT competing here |
| Mid-market | Kapow, Switched On, White Rose | **TARGET POSITION** |
| Premium | Bespoke consultancy, Thinkers in Ed | Workshops only |

### Value Differentiation (Not Price)
1. **Unique content**: Arts + computing integration (no direct competitor)
2. **Practitioner credibility**: Dr. Smith-Nunes' academic credentials
3. **Proven track record**: Google RISE Award, conference presentations
4. **Inclusive pedagogy**: Designed to engage underrepresented groups
5. **Real-world tested**: Developed from actual classroom delivery
