# ReadySaltedCode Relaunch Strategy
## Discussion Document - January 2026

**Purpose**: Help Genevieve decide on products, services, positioning, and language for the ReadySaltedCode relaunch.

**Key Question**: How does ReadySaltedCode make money while positioning Genevieve as a senior industry figure rather than a classroom practitioner?

---

## 1. The Positioning Shift

### Current Positioning (to change)
> "Dr. Genevieve Smith-Nunes, with over 15 years of experience teaching in schools and universities, brings academic rigour to practical classroom resources."

**Problem**: This positions her as "experienced teacher" - someone you hire to run a workshop with your Year 7s.

### Proposed Positioning
> "Dr. Genevieve Smith-Nunes has shaped computing education at the highest levels - from contributing to the UK's national computing curriculum to developing ICT education policy for Caribbean governments. ReadySaltedCode brings this expertise to educators through research-backed resources and methodologies."

**Shift**: From "I'll teach your students" to "I'll empower you to teach your students better"

## 5. datadrivendance - Packaging Options

### Current Positioning
"A series of innovative performance pieces" - positions it as Genevieve's art project.

### Proposed Repositioning
"A proven methodology for combining data, technology, and creative expression" - positions it as something educators can use.

### Package Concept: "datadrivendance for Schools"

**What educators get:**
- Complete session plans (8-12 sessions)
- Technical setup guides (what hardware, what software)
- Data collection frameworks (ethical, age-appropriate)
- Movement/choreography resources (no dance expertise required)
- Culminating event guide (how to run the final performance)
- Student workbooks
- Assessment criteria (links to computing + PE/dance curriculum)

**What makes it special:**
- Cross-curricular (Computing + PE/Dance + Art)
- Inclusive (all abilities can participate)
- Visible outcome (performance event engages parents/community)
- Research-backed (Genevieve's published work)

**Pricing model options:**
- One-time purchase (£199-399?)
- License per school (annual?)
- "Certified facilitator" training (premium, includes support)
