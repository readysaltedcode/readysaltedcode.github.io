# readysaltedcode Revival Strategy
## Business Proposal for Dr. Genevieve Smith-Nunes

**Version 2.0** - Updated with strategic decisions and pricing research

---

## Executive Summary

readysaltedcode has a strong foundation: established brand recognition in STEM education, a Google RISE Award for the datadrivendance project, proven track record delivering workshops to schools and youth organisations, and now backed by Dr. Genevieve Smith-Nunes' academic credentials in data ethics, immersive education, and computing pedagogy.

### Strategic Decisions Made
- **Hero Product**: Licensable resources with freemium model
- **Workshop Capacity**: Maximum 5 days per month (hackday format preferred)
- **Scaling**: Train associates later when demand requires
- **Launch**: Organic soft launch as each product/service is ready

This proposal outlines a strategy to revive readysaltedcode as a sustainable business offering workshops, resources, and training for schools, universities, and youth organisations.

## 2. Service Offerings (Refined Strategy)

**Free Tier** (Lead Generation)
- Sample lesson plans (1-2 per topic area)
- Printable worksheets and activity sheets
- Getting started guides
- Blog posts with teaching ideas
- Newsletter with tips and updates

**Paid Tier 1: Individual Lesson Packs** (£49-79 each)
- Complete lesson pack (e.g., "Wearables with Gemma")
- Teacher guide + student worksheets
- Editable resources (Word/PowerPoint)
- Assessment rubrics

## 10. Summary

### The Strategy
**Freemium resources as the hero product**, supported by limited workshop delivery (max 5 days/month), with organic soft launch as each element is ready.

### Why This Works
1. **Resources scale**: Unlike workshops, digital resources have unlimited capacity
2. **Recurring revenue**: School subscriptions provide predictable income
3. **Workshops build relationships**: Lead to resource sales and testimonials
4. **Low risk**: Soft launch allows iteration before major investment
5. **Leverage existing content**: 6 lessons already on the website to package

### Competitive Position
- **Pricing**: Mid-market (£149-299/year vs Kapow £215-350)
- **Differentiation**: Arts + computing integration (no direct competitor)
- **Credibility**: Google RISE Award + Dr. Smith-Nunes' academic credentials
